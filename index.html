About

My name is Olena Salnikova. I was born and raised in Ukraine and graduated from Kharkiv National University of Radio Electronics (KhNURE), where I gained a strong foundation in information technology and biomedical engineering. After completing my studies, I began my career in IT as a QA Engineer.

Over 8 years of professional experience, I worked on various projects, including web applications, mobile apps, and corporate portals. I was responsible for testing, identifying issues, improving product quality, and collaborating closely with development teams to deliver reliable and high-quality software.

Life circumstances and priorities can change over time. The birth of my two children became an important new chapter in my life, and balancing full-time work with family responsibilities became challenging. As a result, I took a career break. In 2021, my family and I relocated to the United States, which marked a significant transition and a step into the unknown.

Adapting to a new country took time, but now that my children have started school, I am able to focus again on my professional development. I decided to return to the IT industry and continue growing in this field.

Currently, I am studying Intro to Programming at Code the Dream (CTD), where I am building a strong foundation in programming concepts and gaining hands-on experience. This learning path allows me to combine my previous QA and IT background with new technical skills while continuing to learn and grow.

I believe that every stage of life brings valuable experience, and I am motivated to move forward, grow professionally, and build a successful career in the IT industry that has always inspired me.
